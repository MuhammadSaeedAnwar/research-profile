00 / Abstract

Research profile

I work on the security and reliability of large language models — prompt injection, adversarial evaluation, and tool-use safety in LLM agents. My research combines practical cybersecurity experience (network security, penetration testing) with empirical machine-learning evaluation. I built a 250-sample benchmark spanning five prompt-injection attack categories to systematically measure LLM susceptibility to adversarial instructions, with results accepted for presentation at AISec 2026. Current interests: prompt injection defense, LLM agent security, RAG security, and trustworthy AI systems.
